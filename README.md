# Indral

Domain-independent industrial intelligence platform supporting Modbus, OPC UA, MQTT, and BACnet, with temporal graphs, bitemporal history, and Operational Time Travel.

> **Status:** Initial planning. The architecture and development roadmap are being documented; capabilities described here are not yet implemented.

Indral Core is designed to support operational connectivity, historical data, and evidence-backed investigations without assuming a specific industrial domain. Manufacturing/MES and Smart Buildings/BAS are separate planned domain applications.
